CAL-PPO: Fine-Grained Credit Assignment for RL in LLM Reasoning

MSc Dissertation — University of Birmingham, in collaboration with FLAIR Lab, University of Oxford

2025
  • Critic-free PPO that uses a few-shot prompted LLM as a Credit Assignment Oracle to locate the first logical failure in a reasoning trace and apply sparse, token-level penalties.
  • GSM8K pass@1: 59% → 65.25% (Qwen2.5-0.5B + Gemini Oracle). Built on OAT and vLLM.
  • Supervised by Dr Yue Feng (Birmingham); collaboration with FLAIR Lab, University of Oxford.
