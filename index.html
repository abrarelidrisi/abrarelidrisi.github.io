Publications

Representational De-collapse: In-context Overwriting of Supervised Fine-tuning

ICLR 2026, Trustworthy AI for Machine Learning Workshop · UCL Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit

Supervised fine-tuning compresses a model's internal representations in ways that improve task performance but reduce flexibility. This paper shows that in-context evidence conflicting with the fine-tuned behaviour can partially reverse this collapse, measured via layerwise Representational Similarity Analysis on controlled synthetic tasks. The finding has direct implications for alignment: behaviours encoded through SFT are not fixed, and the conditions under which they can be overwritten at inference time are characterisable. The work is ongoing.

Knowledge Distillation of BERT on Arabic (ArabicDistilBERT)

ICLR 2023, Tiny Papers · NeurIPS 2023 Spotlight, North Africans in ML Workshop · Deep Learning Indaba

Arabic NLP has historically lacked efficient, deployable models. This paper produces a distilled Arabic BERT that retains 92.4% of the teacher model's question-answering performance at 70% fewer parameters, making strong Arabic NLP practical in resource-constrained settings. The model has been downloaded over 20,000 times on HuggingFace.

CAL-PPO: Fine-Grained Credit Assignment for RL in LLM Reasoning

MSc Dissertation, University of Birmingham · in collaboration with FLAIR Lab, University of Oxford · supervised by Dr Yue Feng

2025
  • Standard PPO applies a single scalar reward across an entire reasoning trace, penalising correct steps alongside wrong ones. CAL-PPO replaces the value critic with a Credit Assignment Oracle: a two-stage mechanism that first filters obvious errors with a rule-based verifier, then uses a few-shot prompted LLM to locate the first logical failure and apply a sparse, token-level penalty at that point.
  • Improved GSM8K pass@1 from 59% to 65.25% on Qwen2.5-0.5B. Built on the OAT distributed RL framework with vLLM for inference.
